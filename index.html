How we work

AI moves fast. Judgment decides where.

Five stages. AI-assisted implementation compresses the middle; experienced engineering judgment governs the parts where being wrong is expensive.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the actual problem, the constraints, and what "done" means to you.

    HUMAN-LED
  2. 02

    Architect

    Decide the shape: data flow, failure modes, security boundaries, what stays manual.

    HUMAN-LED
  3. 03

    Build

    AI-assisted implementation against the agreed architecture, reviewed every step.

    AI-ACCELERATED
  4. 04

    Validate

    Tests, evals, edge cases, and a security pass before anything touches production.

    AI-ACCELERATED
  5. 05

    Deliver

    Deployed, documented, and handed over so your team can maintain it without me.

    HUMAN-LED

Why the split matters. AI coding assistants are genuinely fast at implementation, and I use them heavily — that's how a fixed-price engagement at this size is possible at all. What they don't do is decide what should be built, where the security boundary sits, or which failure mode will take your business down at 2am. That part is the job, and it doesn't get delegated.

Why SmashGlass

You get the person who does the work.

SmashGlass is a boutique studio, not an agency. There is no bench, no junior team, and no account layer between you and the engineering. You work directly with someone who has run engineering organizations and still writes and ships the code.

That matters most on the decisions that are expensive to get wrong — where the security boundary goes, which integration will quietly break under load, what should stay manual because automating it is a bad trade. Those calls come from having shipped and maintained production systems, not from a framework.

Engagements are fixed-price and scoped up front, typically $1,500–$5,000 for a first project. If it turns into something larger, we'll both know why.
